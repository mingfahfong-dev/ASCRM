How limits are calculated: the source certificates (§8, Uncertainty and Tolerance Intervals) state that a common approach is to set performance gates at 2× or 3× the standard deviation observed during the inter-laboratory certification program, and recommend cross-checking against the ISO 16042 acceptance formula. The bands shown here are certified value ± (k × SD) using the toggle above — they are a calculated convenience, not a separately published "acceptable limit" field in the certificate. The certificates also caution that Table 3 SDs reflect inter-laboratory round-robin variability and do not capture the repeatability of any single instrument — for routine XRF control charting, cross-check against your own lab's historical CRM control-chart limits (ISO 17025) where available. Fe is certified using ISO 2596 moisture-corrected determinations only.

Oxide conversion: Si, Al, Ca, Mg, Ti and K are certified as elemental % in the source documents (Table 3) but are shown here as SiO2, Al2O3, CaO, MgO, TiO2 and K2O to match standard XRF fusion-bead reporting. Certified value and SD were both scaled by the same factor: SiO2 = Si × 2.1390, Al2O3 = Al × 1.8900, CaO = Ca × 1.3992, MgO = Mg × 1.6578, TiO2 = Ti × 1.6680 (factors per this project's XRF workbook 'element conversion' tab), and K2O = K × 1.2045 (standard stoichiometric ratio, not in that tab). Fe, P, S, Mn, LOI and all trace elements remain elemental.

ISO limit: the "ISO" option computes the mass-fraction difference allowance from ISO 16042:2007 Clause 6: Rc = Cs × (−10.771·ln(Cs) + 25.4) / 200, where Cs is the certified mass fraction (%). This clause applies to elemental mass fractions up to 10 %; per Clause 6's note, total Fe instead uses the standard's fixed ±0.5 % Fe allowance regardless of this formula. This mode ignores the Sw/SL toggle above.

PQ (Kim) limit: fixed (not editable) Warning range (Low–High) and Action range (Low Low–High High) for Fe, Al2O3, P, SiO2 and LOI, taken from the green-highlighted cells in ASCRM_New_Limits.xlsx. A measured value inside the Warning range passes, inside the Action range (but outside Warning) is a warning, and outside Action fails. All values in this mode show to 4 decimal places. Elements Kim didn't build a block for (shown in teal, tagged SIM) get an estimated range instead of a real one: her Warning-step ÷ inter-lab-SD ratio is averaged across the 5 elements she did define, then applied to that element's own inter-lab SD. Treat SIM rows as indicative only, not a validated PQ limit.
